Vault paths expected by ESO:
  k8s/tools/database/libredb/app
  k8s/tools/database/libredb/db

Required app keys:
  JWT_SECRET
  ADMIN_EMAIL
  ADMIN_PASSWORD

Required db keys:
  POSTGRES_USER
  POSTGRES_PASSWORD
  POSTGRES_DB

Optional app keys:
  USER_EMAIL
  USER_PASSWORD
  LLM_API_KEY

Optional future OIDC app keys:
  OIDC_CLIENT_ID
  OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET

Seed connections:
  Put non-secret seed structure in values.yaml using ${ENV_VAR} placeholders.
  Credential placeholders are populated by ESO from the existing app/db Vault paths.
